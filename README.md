# 🧠 CureSense – AI Health Assistant

**CureSense** is an AI-powered health assistant built using **Streamlit** and **Google Generative AI (Gemini)**. It offers personalized wellness suggestions and intelligent risk analysis based on user input. Designed to promote proactive health tracking, CureSense empowers users with tailored **lifestyle tips**, **nutrition plans**, **exercise routines**, and even **potential disease or deficiency alerts** — all with a clean and user-friendly interface.

---

## 🔥 Features

- 🔍 **Personalized Lifestyle Recommendations**  
  Get daily health and wellness tips based on symptoms, health conditions, and personal preferences.

- 🍎 **AI-Powered Nutrition Plan**  
  Customized diet advice based on your dietary preferences and nutrition goals.

- 💪 **Exercise Recommendations**  
  Personalized fitness plans depending on your level (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced) and goals.

- 🧬 **Symptom Risk Analyzer**  
  Analyze your symptoms to see if they could lead to serious health issues like **cancer**, **chronic diseases**, or even **nutritional deficiencies**.
